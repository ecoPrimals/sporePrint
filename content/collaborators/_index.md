gen5 is external science production. Collaborators are scientists whose biological
or physical questions the ecosystem serves. The deliverable is *their* science — not
an infrastructure demo. Each collaboration creates new validation targets that
strengthen the springs for every subsequent user.

The feedback loop: collaborator brings a question and domain data, products compose
for their domain, a pseudoSpore is produced (self-verifying artifact), the collaborator
owns the science, results become new spring validation targets, and the ecosystem
strengthens for the next collaborator.
