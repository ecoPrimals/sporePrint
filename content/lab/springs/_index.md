Each spring validates a scientific domain on sovereign hardware — consumer GPUs, no institutional access, no proprietary software. These pages tell each spring's science story: what was reproduced, what was discovered, and what it proved about the infrastructure.

**5 spring hubs** cover the highest-science-output springs. The remaining 3 (neuralSpring, ludoSpring, healthSpring) are documented in the [Spring Catalog](@/architecture/SPRING_CATALOG.md) and will be expanded by upstream contributors following the [content guide](https://github.com/ecoPrimals/wateringHole/blob/main/sporePrint/CONTENT_GUIDE.md).
