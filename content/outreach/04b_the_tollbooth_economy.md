"As a Service" as enclosure — the difference between digging wells and building tollbooths. The essay traces the pattern from river keepers to cloud computing: when you control access to a resource that exists independently of you, you are a tollbooth operator. The answer is not better tollkeepers. The answer is to give everyone a well.

> *"The answer is not to reform the river keeper. The answer is to give everyone a well."*

The tollbooth pattern appears in [detroit.primals.eco](https://detroit.primals.eco) as charter school management companies: The Purpose Group, LLC positions itself between public funding and children's education, extracting [72.67% of spending](https://detroit.primals.eco/analysis/funding-flow/) through a management agreement. The school is the river. The management company is the tollbooth.
