## Two Voices

ecoPrimals speaks in two registers. The builder shows the work. The philosopher names why it matters.

| Voice | Register | Publishes as |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **ecoPrimal** | Builder — specs, tests, hardware, deploy paths | Technical articles, partnership briefs |
| **attsi** | Philosopher — structures, preconditions, critique | Essays, structural analysis |

Each references the other. ecoPrimal carries the weight. attsi carries the meaning. Neither is enough alone.

## The Relationship to atlasHugged

The [atlasHugged essays](@/philosophy/_index.md) are the philosophical foundation. The outreach articles are the public-facing translation. atlasHugged asks *why*. The outreach articles show *what* and invite *who*.

The articles can be read without atlasHugged. atlasHugged can be read without the articles. Each stands alone. Each is stronger with the other.
