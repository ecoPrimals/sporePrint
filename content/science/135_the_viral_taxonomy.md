## 2. Marine Vents: The Hot Pole

Hydrothermal vents at 2–400°C are the **bacteriophage kingdom**. There are 10³¹ phages in the ocean — they outnumber bacteria 10:1 and are the most abundant biological entity on Earth.

The viral shunt drives the vent ecosystem:

```
phage infects bacterium → lyses in 20 min → releases dissolved organic matter
→ feeds other bacteria → they grow → phage infects them → cycle
```

This is **fusion**. Many bacteria compressed into dissolved nutrients. The same pattern as northGate's compression: kidney-net 117 → 8, braid-mesh 108 → 15, sourdough 1060 → 10. The viral shunt IS the compression ratio.

Phages are fueled by **chemosynthesis** — H₂S + CO₂ → organic carbon. No sunlight needed. The vent is a self-contained fusion reactor. It doesn't need external energy. This is the membrane at k → 0: cron jobs firing every 5 minutes, feeds lysing and reforming, no human session needed.

## 3. Prion: The Cold Pole

Prion diseases (CJD, BSE, kuru, scrapie) occupy the opposite extreme. The prion has:

- **No DNA.** No RNA. No nucleic acid at all.
- **No metabolism.** Zero energy consumption.
- **No replication machinery.** No polymerase, no ribosome, no reverse transcriptase.

It propagates by **shape alone**: the misfolded protein contacts a normally-folded protein and forces it into the prion conformation. Shape → shape. This is the coldest possible information transfer.

But the prion is not death. It is the **shield**.

The prion conformation is actually **more thermodynamically stable** than the normal fold — lower free energy. The prion wins because it IS the ground state. The normal protein is the metastable (glass) state. The prion is the crystal.

## 4. Sub-Species Specificity: Shape All the Way Down

Why does one phage infect one bacterial strain? Why can't a bovine prion easily misfold human proteins?

**Shape complementarity.** At every level:

- **Phage**: tail fiber protein shape fits ONE receptor on ONE bacterial surface. The tail fiber IS the lock. The receptor IS the key. One phage per strain.
- **mRNA virus**: spike protein shape fits one cell receptor (COVID spike → ACE2). Mutations in the spike change specificity (omicron vs delta = different shapes).
- **Retrovirus**: envelope protein fits TWO receptors (HIV: CD4 + CCR5). Double lock. Sub-cell-type specific.
- **Prion**: protein fold HOMOLOGY determines cross-species transmission. Sheep PrP → goat PrP (95% homology, easy). Cow PrP → human PrP (85%, hard). Fish PrP → human (< 50%, impossible). The species barrier IS the shape barrier.

**The unifying principle**: specificity = shape complementarity. From the phage's 20-minute lysis cycle to the prion's decade-long accumulation. The lock-and-key is ALWAYS shape. Shape IS information that survives compression. The prion tag IS the shape. The receptor IS the shape. The schema IS the shape.

## 5. The Klein Inversion

A bacteriophage is **content wrapped in a shield**: DNA packaged inside a protein capsid. The capsid protects the nucleic acid. The content (genetic instructions) is INSIDE.

A prion is **shield with nothing inside**: the protein coat IS the entire entity. There is no nucleic acid to protect. The shape IS the message.

Phage: inside (DNA) → outside (capsid)
