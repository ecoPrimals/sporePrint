# Paper 139: Fruit vs Mycelium

## Abstract

We named Kingdom Fungi on the **fruiting bodies we can see** — sporePrint, sourDough, rhizoCrypt, the JSON feeds that erupt into public view. That taxonomy may be **way off**. The living fungus is mostly **mycelium**: the underground mesh. In the membrane, mycelium is UDS sockets, WireGuard keepalives, unpublished data trails, cron hyphae — not the paper that fruits. Many clinical antifungals are also **neurotoxins** (or neurotoxic at therapeutic margins). That is not coincidence: fungal membranes and neural membranes share sterol chemistry, mitochondria, and ion-channel logic. Shared toxicity is shared biology. We propose the **antifungal↔neurotoxin coupling** as a new dataset for both pharmacology and membrane immunity — and we publish next steps to classify mycelium before we trust the fruit names.

## 3. Antifungals Are Neurotoxins — The Coupled Dataset

### 3.1 Clinical pattern

Many agents used against fungi hit the nervous system:

- **Azoles** (fluconazole, voriconazole, itraconazole): CYP-mediated interactions; voriconazole — visual hallucinations, neuropathy
- **Amphotericin B**: membrane pores; systemic toxicity; CNS risk when barriers fail
- **Flucytosine**: crosses into CNS; neurotoxicity at high levels
- **Griseofulvin / older agents**: documented CNS effects in historical use
- Even “safer” echinocandins remind us: selective toxicity is a gradient, not a wall

### 3.2 Why the overlap exists

Fungi and neurons are not siblings by taxonomy, but they are **cousins by membrane physics**:

1. **Sterols** — ergosterol (fungi) vs cholesterol (animals). Azoles target ergosterol synthesis; host cholesterol pathways and neurosteroids sit next door.
2. **Mitochondria** — shared electron-transport vulnerability; ROS and ATP failure look like “neurotoxicity” in high-demand tissue.
3. **Ion gradients** — membrane potential is the currency of both hyphal tip growth and action potentials.
4. **BBB windows** — circumventricular organs and lipophilic drugs: what clears a fungus in tissue may cross into CSF.

**Hypothesis:** the set of compounds that are *both* antifungal and neurotoxic is a **natural experiment** on shared membrane targets. The intersection is a dataset.
