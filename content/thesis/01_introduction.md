## The Central Question

> *Do environmental constraints merely filter existing solutions, or do they actively reshape the fitness landscape — driving specialization toward constraint-specific optima through independent evolutionary trajectories? And if so, does this principle extend from biological evolution to computational system development?*

*Thermus aquaticus* evolved heat-stable DNA polymerase not because heat resistance was selected from a pre-existing toolkit, but because thermal constraint reshaped the fitness landscape so deeply that the only reachable optima were heat-stable. The constraint did not accelerate the search — it changed what was searchable.

This thesis argues the same principle operates when AI-assisted software development is constrained by a strong type system: Rust's compile-time selection pressure reshapes the solution space in ways that unconstrained development (runtime testing only) cannot replicate.
