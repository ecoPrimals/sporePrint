## Nature's Strategy for Hard Problems

Hard problems are solved by building **generators** and letting selection verify output — enzymes, genomes, immune systems. Not by deriving solutions from first principles.

The constrained evolution methodology (AI generates, Rust compiler verifies, developer selects) is a direct instance of this accept-and-generate strategy. The distinction is empirical observation, not abstract P vs NP.
