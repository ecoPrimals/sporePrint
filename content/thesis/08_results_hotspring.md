## Computational Plasma Physics

hotSpring validates BarraCuda against plasma physics, nuclear structure, lattice QCD, spectral theory, and neuromorphic computing. 197+ checks pass across 39 suites and ~48 experiments at ~$0.80 compute cost. Five silent bugs in upstream Sarkas MD were found and patched.

Domains covered: Sarkas Yukawa MD, nuclear EOS (AME2020), SU(3) lattice QCD, Anderson/Hofstadter spectral methods, and NPU pipelines.
