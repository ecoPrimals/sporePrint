## Precision Agriculture

airSpring validates precision agriculture methods: FAO-56 Penman-Monteith and alternative ET calculations, soil moisture sensor calibration (Dong et al. 2020, 2024), and water-balance scheduling. 3,123+ checks pass across Python, Rust, cross-validation, and GPU phases.

Real-data validation on 15,300 station-days from 100 Michigan stations produces R² = 0.967.
