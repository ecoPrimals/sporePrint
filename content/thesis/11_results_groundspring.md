## Measurement Noise & Uncertainty

groundSpring quantifies measurement noise, error propagation, and uncertainty across ten domains: agricultural sensors, meteorology, microbiome sequencing, geophysics, spectral theory, eco-evolutionary dynamics, inverse problems, warm dense matter, and immunological physics.

376 checks across 33 experiments. groundSpring serves as the **uncertainty foundation** — its tolerances inform the acceptance criteria for every other spring.
