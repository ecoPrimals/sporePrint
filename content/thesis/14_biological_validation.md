## The LTEE Sequencing Proposal

Constrained evolution predicts shared statistical signatures: convergent solutions, power-law fitness, hitchhikers, historical contingency. Prior chapters provide computational evidence. Lenski's **LTEE frozen fossil record** — 75,000+ generations, 12 populations, Michigan State University — is the ideal biological dataset to test these predictions.

Proposed analyses:
- Convergent mutation classification across populations
- Hitchhiker/neutral drift quantification
- Temporal specialization dynamics
- Comparison with computational convergence patterns from the ecoPrimals codebase
