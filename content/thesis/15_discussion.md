## Synthesis and Limitations

This chapter addresses three questions:

1. **Is this just good engineering?** — The alternative explanation that any competent developer would produce similar results regardless of constraint. The NTT→FFT evidence and convergent IPC suggest otherwise, but the question is addressed honestly.

2. **The fastidiousness trade-off** — Constrained evolution produces specialists. The cost is reduced generality. When does constraint become pathological? Muller's ratchet provides the boundary condition.

3. **Broader implications** — If constraints reshape fitness landscapes in both biology and computation, what does this suggest for research methodology, scientific reproducibility, and the design of development environments?
